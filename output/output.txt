Usagi oishi kano yama
兎追いし　かの山
I used to chase rabbits on that mountain

Kobuna tsurishi kano kawa
小鮒釣りし　かの川
I used to fish for small crucian carp in that river

Yume wa ima mo megurite
夢は今も　めぐりて
Even now, the dreams go round and round

Wasuregataki furusato
忘れがたき　故郷
My unforgettable hometown

Ikani imasu chichi-haha
いかにいます　父母
How are my father and mother doing?

tsutsuganashi ya tomogaki
つつが　なしや　友がき
Are my old friends well?

ame ni kaze ni tsuketemo
雨に風に　つけても
Whenever it rains or the wind blows

omoiizuru furusato
思い出づる　故郷
I remember my hometown

Kokorozashi o hatashite
志を　はたして
Having fulfilled my aspirations

itsunohinika kaeran
いつの日にか　帰らん
Someday, I shall return

yama wa aoki furusato
山はあおき　故郷
My hometown, where the mountains are green

mizu wa kiyoki furusato
水は清き　故郷
My hometown, where the waters are clear
